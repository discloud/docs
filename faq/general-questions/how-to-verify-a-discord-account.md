# How to verify a Discord account?

To use the **Discloud Bot** and manage your applications from Discord, you need to **verify your account** on the Discloud Discord server. Only then do you get access to the commands channel.

***

## ✅ Verifying your account

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Join the [**Discloud Discord Server**](https://discord.discloudbot.com/).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Follow the instructions in the verification channel.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Once verified, you will be assigned the **`Verified en-us`** role.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
With your account verified, you gain access to the [**`#🔌・commands`**](https://discord.com/channels/584490943034425391/1051126795883261962) channel, where you can use the bot's commands to manage your applications.
{% endhint %}

***
